## The letter

Subject:

```text
[XR/CT Imaging Research] Request for the Harms Study Group registry radiographs
```

Body:

```text
Dear <the name the introduction gives>,

I study the application of computer science to improving spinal health, and my current work needs standing radiographs of the spine, taken at many sites on many devices, in which the vertebrae and the operated levels are already known. Dr Ali Uneri of Johns Hopkins suggested the Harms Study Group's registries and kindly made this introduction. Your site describes the adolescent idiopathic scoliosis registry as about twenty years of prospective, multicentre surgical cases from 37 member surgeons, with the cerebral palsy, Scheuermann's kyphosis and Marfan registries beside it.

My work measures how reliably automated image-analysis methods recognise which vertebra is which on radiographs, including the transitional variants that cause wrong-level errors in spine care, across devices and exposures, reported as per-image agreement with expert labels and confidence intervals, by site and by device.

I am writing to ask whether the de-identified radiographs themselves, the standing coronal and lateral films before and after surgery in their original resolution, with the instrumented levels and the measurements that belong to each film, can be shared with a research user, and on what terms: through the Database User Program or otherwise, under what agreement, and at what cost. I am at the Department of General Surgery, Wayne State University School of Medicine and the Detroit Medical Center; the images would be held on Wayne State's research computing storage, read only by the study team, never redistributed, and deleted at the end of the project if you ask; the work is on de-identified imaging under Wayne State IRB determination HPR 2026-269. I will sign a data use agreement through Wayne State on your terms, follow the group's publication policy, cite the Harms Study Group in anything we publish, and share our results on your images with you before publication.

With thanks,

Gregory J. Schwing, MD, PhD
Department of General Surgery
Wayne State University School of Medicine / Detroit Medical Center
Detroit, Michigan, USA
go2432@wayne.edu
+1 504-994-0403
```

What to expect back: whether images are shared at all (the site names only a database and measurements), the program's terms (a research-tool fee of USD 5,000 a year is listed), and an agreement, all the owner's to decide and sign; then a transfer by the route the group gives, fetched as a grid job on the owner's word.
