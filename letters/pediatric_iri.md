Dear Dr Wang and Dr Xu,

I study the application of computer science to improving spinal health, and my current work needs fluoroscopic and angiographic image sequences in children from a modern C-arm system, which none of the sets I hold cover. Your IRI-VFI dataset (IEEE DataPort, doi:10.21227/v4kg-zp42; Tang, Xiong, Wu et al., "Radiation reduction for interventional radiology imaging: a video frame interpolation solution") provides 1,357 sequences and 95,308 images in angiography, fluoroscopy and subtraction modes from the Children's Hospital of Chongqing Medical University.

My work measures how reliably automated image-analysis methods recognise spinal anatomy on fluoroscopy across devices, exposures and patient ages, reported as per-image agreement with expert labels and confidence intervals. The spine is in view in many abdominal interventional sequences, so your images would extend that measurement to paediatric patients and to the ARTIS icono system.

I was not able to obtain the files through IEEE DataPort, so I am writing to ask whether the sequences could be shared for non-commercial research, and what terms you require.
