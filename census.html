Spine Data Census

Every dataset of OpenSpineConsortium/spine-datasets counted from its one record, written on 2026-10-25. The census reads the records alone, never a dataset's files, and folds in only the records that are new or changed since its last run, so adding a dataset is adding its record. Counts are what each record states or what its landing pass measured; a figure counts only the sets whose record gives it. The CT page lists every record that holds CT by region, with the masks each set has.
