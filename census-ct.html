Spine CT Census

Every record of OpenSpineConsortium/spine-datasets that holds CT, counted from its record, written on 2026-10-25: the body regions its field of view covers, the masks the set has, who made them, whether they name the vertebrae by level and where they are on the grid. A set's masks are what its record's segmentations say, from the data platform's manifest, IDC's own index and the grid; the source's own masks and those made on the grid are kept apart. The whole census counts every dataset.
